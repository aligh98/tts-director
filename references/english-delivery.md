# English for the ear

The rules are the same as in Persian (`persian-delivery.md`): spoken register, short sentences, the punch word last, interjections over tags, and punctuation as the score. This file lists only what differs in English.

## Register

- Use contractions everywhere: "you're", "don't", "it's", "here's". Uncontracted text sounds read.
- Talk to one viewer: "you", "your account". Never "users" or "one".
- Spell out what the model might misread: "two-point-five X", "AI" → "A.I.", and a URL in full ("example dot com slash deals").

## Emphasis

- English has capitals, and ElevenLabs uses them as emphasis: "This is the ONLY way." Use one capitalised word per few sentences at most. A capitalised sentence reads as shouting.
- Italics and bold do nothing. Emphasis comes from word order, capitals, a short line on its own, or "..." before the word.

## Interjections

| Want | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | "Wait.", "Okay, this is wild.", "No way." |
| Attention | "Look.", "Here's the thing...", "Listen." |
| Turn | "So...", "Now...", "Oh, and..." |
| Irony | "Sure, just wait until the last day." (+ `[sarcastic]` or `[chuckles]`) |

Never write "haha" or "lol". Use `[laughs]` or `[chuckles]`.

## Before and after

> Our sale ends on Friday and will not be repeated.

> [excited] Okay, this ends FRIDAY... and it's not coming back!

> It is recommended to keep your battery between 20 and 80 percent.

> [whispers] Here's the trick... keep it between twenty and eighty. That's it.
